## Executive Summary
Tokenizing Real World Assets (RWA) without verified off-chain integrity creates systemic risk ("Toxic RWAs"). While blockchain protocols enforce on-chain compliance (KYC/AML), they fail to verify the physical status, legal ownership, or structural validity of the underlying asset. 

**Prop Trust Verified (PTVS)** introduces a judicial-grade physical oracle framework, connecting verified judicial experts (PTCE) to smart contracts.

## Key Pillars of the Ecosystem

### 1. The Judicial Physical Oracle
- Independent verification performed by accredited judicial experts (*Peritos Judiciales*).
- Cryptographic tying of reports using SHA-256 hashes, ORCID IDs, and professional registry numbers.

### 2. Legal & Multi-Jurisdictional Architecture
- **Matriz Global:** Aurema Group LLC (Delaware) for IP governance and capital raising.
- **Operations:** Aurema Digital Assets OÜ (Estonia) operating under MiCA EU framework.
- **Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs):** Isolated asset-holding entities with SAFE and Escrow milestone releases.

### 3. Native ERC-3643 Integration
PTVS claims directly feed into ONCHAINID identity registries, enabling automated compliance checks (e.g., blocking token transfers if physical audit credentials expire or fail).
